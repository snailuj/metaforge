## Operator Interpretation (post-run)

The auto-ratified "non-regressive" verdict is **misleading** — all 9 cells
and the baseline scored zero or negative separation. The Lakoff cohort
exposes a structural finding the M04 v1 MUNCH sweep couldn't surface.

### Three signals worth recording

1. **Cosine path generates Lakoff candidates but they don't reach the output.**
   `embedding: 0` and `both: 0` across every cell — yet the M04 v1 canary test
   (`TestCascadeUnion_ClassicalPairsSurface_AsCandidates`) proves anger→fire,
   idea→light, time→money, truth→hammer surface as candidates under
   `EmbeddingTopK=10000`. The sweep uses TopK=200, but the deeper issue is
   that even when candidates reach the scoring loop, the M03 cascade
   (jaccard_salience + capped cosine reward, DCap=0.77) ranks them below
   within-domain neighbours. `/forge/suggest?limit=100` returns the top-100
   by `final_score`, and classical-metaphor pairs don't make that cut.

2. **Some cells score NEGATIVE separation.** Cells dmin0.4_dmax0.* produce
   separation around -0.27, meaning the (few) inapt vehicles that scored
   outranked the (few) apt vehicles. With ~12-16 apt and 1-2 inapt scoring
   per cell, the sample is too small to draw a strong conclusion, but the
   sign tells us: when inapt vehicles happen to share a cluster_id with
   the topic (e.g. "anger" and "doormat" both have a "tense" property?),
   the structural overlap reward is incidental rather than meaningful.

3. **Cluster path catches a few Lakoff pairs (6-16/80).** Cases where the
   topic and vehicle happen to share a curated cluster_id — likely
   anger↔fire (both share "burning"), idea↔light (both share "bright"),
   etc. These are the Lakoff pairs M04 v1's binary-presence canary
   doesn't fully credit to the embedding path; they were already
   surfacing via cluster path at M03.

### Root cause: scoring math is type-agnostic

The M03 cascade scorer uses `jaccard_salience` (set-intersection over
shared cluster_ids weighted by salience) + cosine rerank. **Both signals
reward within-domain similarity.** Classical metaphors are
cross-domain by definition — their shared properties span multiple
*types* (sensorimotor + behaviour + effect for anger↔fire) but the
*count* of shared clusters is lower than for within-domain paraphrases.

The Lakoff cohort cannot succeed against M04's scoring math regardless
of `(d_min, d_max)` tuning. The fix isn't in M04. It's M05's
type-aligned structural matching — rewarding diversity of types in the
overlap, not just the size of the overlap.

### Operator verdict: keep `ModeCluster` default; promote M05

- **No code change to `DefaultCascadeConfig`.** `ModeCluster` remains
  the production default. Users opt into union/embedding via the
  `METAFORGE_FORGE_CANDIDATES` env var or `--candidate-sources` flag.
- **M04 v1 + v2 final verdict:** the cosine-band candidate generator IS
  working (canary test verified), but downstream scoring math doesn't
  preferentially rank cross-domain metaphors. Shipping union mode by
  default would not improve user-facing aptness on the Lakoff cohort
  AND would cost ~5× per-request latency (from M04 v1 measurements).
- **What M04 v1 ships:** observability infrastructure (anomaly aggregator,
  source-tagged candidates, per-request mode dispatch), the cosine-scan
  primitive, the canary test that pins generation behaviour. These are
  foundational for M05 — the type-aligned scorer will sit on top.
- **What M04 v2 has now answered:** the cosine-band hypothesis alone
  isn't sufficient. Type-aware scoring is required to convert
  generation lift into ranking lift. This validates M05's premise.
